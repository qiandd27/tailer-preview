Marine power.
From steering to deck.

Steering gear and deck machinery in electro-hydraulic, electric and self-contained configurations, selected around vessel, load and drive requirements.

Two product groups. Five configurations.

Marine steering gear

Marine steering gear covering electro-hydraulic and motor-control configurations. The brochure lists a steering-torque range of 33–1030 ton·m; controls, system configuration and vessel requirements require project-specific confirmation.

Marine deck machinery

Deck machinery for marine anchoring and mooring, including electro-hydraulic, electric and electro-hydraulic self-contained configurations. Selection depends on vessel, controls and installation requirements; features vary by configuration.
