Selection guide

Common questions.

How do I identify my component?

Provide the equipment brand, machine model, complete nameplate code and product photos. Similar series may have different controls, connections and mounting dimensions.

Is a series name enough to confirm compatibility?

A series name is only a starting point. Displacement, working pressure, controls, rotation, connections and duty must also be checked.

Where can I find structures and parts information?

Enquire with the complete model, part reference or nameplate photograph. We will identify the appropriate documents. Catalogues are supplied on request.

How can I make an enquiry?

Fill in your machine, product and requirements on the contact page. You can copy or download the enquiry, or open it in your email application.
