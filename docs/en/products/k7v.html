Seven K7V sizes

Catalogue displacements are 65 cm³/rev for K7V63, 130 for K7V125 and 242 for K7V240; the model number is not always the displacement. Tandem pumps offer optional PTO for excavators and other open circuits.

Regulator and construction

The leaflet lists constant and total horsepower, high-pressure cut-off and variable horsepower controls, alongside manual, positive, negative, two-stage maximum flow, load-sensing and electric flow options. The catalogue describes a one-piece casing, insert-type tilting pin and separable support.
