M5X motor series

M5X is the hydraulic motor component, including M5X80, M5X130, M5X180 and M5X250. It integrates relief, make-up and anti-reaction valves plus a mechanical parking brake. Compare displacement, pressure, speed and mass in the motor table.

FRG reducer series

FRG is the two-stage planetary reducer component. The matching table lists RG06D, RG08D, RG11D, RG12E, RG14D, RG16E, RG20D, RG23E and RG27D, retaining the source RG codes. Reducers can be requested separately; confirm ratio, output torque and dimensions for each motor/reducer pair.

Reading the complete code

In the sample code M5X130CHB-10/***-FRG11, M5X denotes the series, 130 the displacement code, CH the valve-cover code, B the built-in parking brake, 10 the male spline code, *** the relief setting, and FRG the reducer family. The example is not a complete ordering specification.
