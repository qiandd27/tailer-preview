FSM motor series

The catalogue lists FSM34-R02D and FSM54-R04D assemblies with 33.5 and 53.5 cm³ motor configurations. Integrated relief, make-up, anti-reaction, parking-brake and delay functions serve catalogue applications in 5–8 t excavators.

View specifications / configuration →

RD reducer and assembly configurations

The integrated two-stage planetary reducer uses needle bearings and hydraulic-oil lubrication. The source lists ratios of 19.464 and 22.689; assemblies sharing a model name can still have different displacement and pressure configurations.
