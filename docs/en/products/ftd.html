Nine FTD travel assemblies

From FTD26 to FTD220, the catalogue covers applications from 12 to 100 t. Output torque, assembly speed, motor pressure and brake torque vary by model and are compared below. The FTD family name alone is insufficient for selection.

View specifications / configuration →

Motor, reducer and two-speed control

A swash-plate piston motor drives a two-stage planetary reducer with relief, counterbalance and mechanical parking-brake functions and high/low-speed shifting. Catalogue applications include excavators, drilling rigs and crawler cranes. Confirm hub/frame interfaces, ports and controls.
